<div class="kicker">Recommendation</div>

<ul style="font-size:1.2rem; line-height:1.7">
  <li><b>Commit infra reservations</b> — cut cloud unit cost ~20%.</li>
  <li><b>Freeze support hiring</b> — until utilization exceeds 75%.</li>
  <li><b>Add FX hedging</b> — on EU-billed contracts.</li>
</ul>

<div class="takeaway" style="margin-top:1.2rem">Target: recover <b>4 of the 6</b> lost margin points by Q1.</div>

## The driver
- Cloud cost up 52%, ahead of usage
- Support headcount up 40%, low utilization
- FX swing on EU contracts (~1.5 pts)

::right::

## The action
- Commit infra reservations → ~20% lower unit cost
- Freeze hiring until utilization > 75%
- Hedge EU-billed contracts to remove FX swing
